## Abstract

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Mushroom edibility is increasing rapidly. Nowadays, food markets are highly concentrating on mushrooms and provide various edible dishes related to mushrooms. Mushrooms can be binarily categorized as edible and poisonous mushrooms. Classification of mushrooms requires high precision and accuracy. This paper primarily focuses on the binary classification of mushrooms using various parameters such as cap color, odor, and habitat related to mushrooms. Various machine learning classifiers like Random Forests (RF), Naïve Bayes (NB), Logistic Regression (LR), Decision Trees (DT), K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN), ANN, and Support Vector Classifier (SVC) were implemented for classification using K folds and Grid Search based on various correlation matrices and dimensionality reduction techniques like Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and Kernel PCA. In terms of results, ANN and KNN outperformed all other algorithms in terms of performance metrics by achieving almost 100% accuracy. This real-time model deployment can lead to building a classifier model for mushroom edibility classification which will be beneficial for chefs and culinarians.
</p>
