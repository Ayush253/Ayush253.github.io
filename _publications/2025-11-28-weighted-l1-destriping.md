## Abstract

<p style="text-align: justify;">
Stripe noise is a common artifact in remote sensing imagery, particularly in data from push-broom or whisk-broom sensors. These structured vertical streaks degrade radiometric quality and impair tasks such as landcover classification and change detection. While deep learning methods show promise, they rely on large annotated datasets and generalize poorly to unseen data. Convex optimization offers interpretability and robustness, but existing formulations often impose uniform column-wise sparsity, leading to oversmoothing. This paper proposes a novel convex destriping method based on weighted ℓ1 regularization with pixel-level sparsity via column-adaptive weights. An efficient reweighted alternating direction method of multipliers solver is then introduced. Experiments on simulated and real satellite images demonstrate consistent improvements over existing destriping techniques.
</p>
